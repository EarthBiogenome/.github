# Earth Biogenome Project (EBP)

The Earth BioGenome Project aims to sequence, catalog, and characterize
the genomes of all of Earth's ~2 million described eukaryotic species
within 10 years.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-earthbiogenome.org-blue)](https://www.earthbiogenome.org)
[![GoaT](https://img.shields.io/badge/Assemblies-GoaT%20Database-green)](https://goat.genomehubs.org)

---

## What We Do

- **Coordinate** genome sequencing across 60+ affiliate projects worldwide
- **Avoid duplication** by tracking sequencing status network-wide
- **Maximize coverage** by prioritizing phylogenetically significant species
- **Share tools** that help the network sequence smarter, not just faster

---

## Affiliate Network

EBP brings together major biodiversity genomics initiatives including
DToL, ERGA, VGP, and many more — each contributing to a shared
goal of comprehensive genome coverage across the tree of life.

→ Full affiliate list at [earthbiogenome.org](https://www.earthbiogenome.org/affiliated-project-networks)
